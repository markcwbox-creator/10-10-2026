7. STATISTICAL ANALYSIS
 H0: Sucrose concentration has no effect on % mass change (all solutions have the same mean % change).
 Ha: Sucrose concentration affects % mass change (at least one differs).
 alpha = 0.05, df = (6 solutions - 1) = 5, critical X^2 = 11.07
 Chi-square (O = class mean % change, E = average of the 6 means = 20.50):
  X^2 = sum (O-E)^2/E
   Clear   (3.74-20.50)^2/20.50  = 13.70
   Violet  (11.85-20.50)^2/20.50 =  3.65
   Cerulean(17.53-20.50)^2/20.50 =  0.43
   Salmon  (25.37-20.50)^2/20.50 =  1.16
   Yellow  (29.68-20.50)^2/20.50 =  4.11
   Green   (34.85-20.50)^2/20.50 = 10.04
   X^2 = 33.09 > 11.07, P = 3.6e-6 < 0.05
 Conclusion: reject H0. Sucrose concentration significantly affects % mass change.
 Cross-check, one-way ANOVA across the 6 known solutions: F = 84.3, P < 0.001 (same conclusion).
